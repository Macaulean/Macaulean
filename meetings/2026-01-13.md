### Polynomial Ideal Checking Implementation

The team discussed implementing polynomial ideal membership checking using Macaulay 2, focusing on how to handle polynomial expressions and local variables. They explored the possibility of converting statements to the rationals and agreed to separate local variables that are zero into generators for the ideal. Jay raised a design question about whether to create a separate ideal object in Macaulay 2 or handle everything as a single ideal membership query. The team decided to start with sending everything in one block for simplicity, but Jay suggested considering a more complex API in the future that could handle iterative operations and remember Gröbner bases.

### Expanding Beyond McCulloch 2 Approach

The team discussed generalizing their approach beyond McCulloch 2 and considered reusing computations for subsequent tasks. They debated the necessity of strictly following the Merdy standard, with Jay suggesting they might move away from it if it doesn't serve their purposes. Damiano and Jay agreed to focus on specific goals, such as polynomial factorization and reducing coefficients modulo an ideal, with Damiano highlighting the need for an oracle to determine reductions. Matthew shared his progress on analyzing an example, noting the need to understand the algorithm better to optimize computations. The team also considered their scope in relation to Gröbner basis computations, questioning whether they should aim to be faster than Gröbner or reduce problems to a form Gröbner might solve.

### Polynomial Identity Verification Strategies

The team discussed strategies for verifying polynomial identities, particularly focusing on using Gröbner bases and Macaulay 2 for checking ideal membership. They explored various approaches to verify large polynomial identities, including coefficient comparison, derivative evaluation, and point sampling methods. Jay and Damiano agreed to extend the irreducible tactic to work over more general polynomial rings, while Jay will work on implementing the ideal membership code in Macaulay 2. The team decided to focus on making it easy for Macaulay 2 and Lean to communicate, rather than building all verification backends in Lean. They agreed to meet again the following week and to collect and share additional polynomial problems that could be solved using their approach.
